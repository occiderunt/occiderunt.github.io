Bling

Chat that goes straight across.

A peer-to-peer messenger built on libp2p. Two machines talk over an encrypted connection and there is no room server reading along. Pair once with a QR code and the app remembers the peer, even after a restart.

NET Study Synopsis

One event, four witnesses.

The four Gospels in parallel columns, one passage at a time, with shared wording lit up between them. 253 pericopes from the Prologue to the Ascension. Keyboard-first on the desktop, swipe-first on the phone, and it reads offline.

Shimondle

A quiet front end for local models.

Desktop chat for Ollama with streaming thoughts, model switching, and zero telemetry. The model already runs on your laptop; Shimondle just gets out of its way. Built as shimondle-ai, a small studio for local-first AI.

about

Hi, I'm
Occiderunt.

Student and developer. I like AI, specifically agentic frameworks: systems that can actually do things, not just talk about them.

That interest keeps pulling me down the stack to the parts I find interesting: the network layer, the local model, the file on your disk. Bling exists because a chat app should not need a company in the middle. Synopsis exists because the Gospels are easier to see side by side. Shimondle exists because the model already runs on your laptop.

Everything here is free, open source, and built to work without an account. The name is Latin for they killed. Make of that what you will.
